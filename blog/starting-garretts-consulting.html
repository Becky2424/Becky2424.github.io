Announcing Garrett’s Marketing & AI Business Consulting

February 27, 2026

I am thrilled to officially announce the launch of Garrett’s Marketing & AI Business Consulting — a firm built on years of hands-on business experience and a genuine passion for helping companies work smarter with AI.

This has been a long time coming. For those who know my story, you know I have spent years in the trenches — managing teams, launching facilities, driving revenue, and solving the kinds of messy operational problems that do not come with a playbook. Now I am channeling all of that experience into something new.

Why Now?

AI is no longer a future concept. It is here, and it is transforming how businesses operate every single day. But here is the problem I keep seeing: most businesses know they should be using AI, but they do not know where to start or what will actually move the needle.

That is exactly the gap I am here to fill. I am not just someone who studied AI in a classroom. I have supported a 13-location district, built marketing campaigns from scratch, launched facilities from the ground up, and led teams across three different industries. I understand how businesses actually run — and that is what makes my consulting different.

Where It All Started

Long before AI entered the picture, I was running Garrett’s Marketing LLC — a freelance marketing consultancy where I designed brochures, flyers, websites, and full marketing campaigns for small business clients. I built that business through cold calling, community outreach, and pure hustle.

That entrepreneurial foundation never left me. Even as I moved into operations and management roles in self-storage and hospitality, I carried that marketing mindset and client-first approach everywhere I went. Now, with the launch of Garrett’s Marketing & AI Business Consulting, I am bringing it all full circle.

What I Offer

My focus is on practical, high-impact AI solutions — especially for multi-facility operators in the self-storage industry. I help businesses identify where AI can create real value, not just hype. Some of the areas I specialize in include:

  • Dynamic pricing engines that adjust rates based on demand and competition
  • AI-powered chat and phone agents that handle inquiries around the clock
  • Centralized dashboards for real-time visibility across every facility
  • Predictive maintenance and staffing to reduce costs and downtime
  • Automated collections that improve cash flow without the headaches

But my services are not limited to self-storage. Any business looking to streamline operations, improve customer experience, or scale without adding headcount — I can help.

The Road Ahead

Launching this firm is more than a career move for me. It is the culmination of everything I have built — from my early days designing flyers for local businesses, to managing districts and training teams, to now helping companies harness the power of AI.

The best consultants are not the ones who just know the technology. They are the ones who understand the business problems the technology is meant to solve.

That is the principle I am building this firm on. If you are curious about what AI could do for your business, I would love to have that conversation. Let us figure out what is possible — together.
